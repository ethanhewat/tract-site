The problem with solar leads

Most solar leads cost you a surveyor's time before they cost you anything else.

Solar installer lead generation is usually sold by the lead or by the click. The risk of a bad lead, a no-show or a tyre-kicker sits with you.

01

Shared leads

Leads from comparison sites are often resold to several installers, so you are racing others to the same phone call.

02

Surveys that don't happen

A no-show still takes a surveyor and a slot in the diary. Lead agencies bill for leads, not surveys, so the cost of a no-show is yours.

03

Ads with no follow-up

A homeowner who fills in a form and waits two days has usually spoken to someone else. Speed to the first call decides who gets the survey.

Qualified solar leads

Every homeowner is vetted by phone before they reach your diary.

Tell Tract your area and what else you install, such as batteries or EV chargers, and the call is briefed around your business.

On the call, the homeowner confirms

  • They own the property. Tenants and people renting a flat don't get booked in.
  • They make the decision, or the person who does will be in the room.
  • They live in your area, checked against the postcodes you actually cover.
  • They know what a survey is: a site visit to assess the roof and the home, not a quote over the phone.

Who this works for

  • You have surveyor capacity now and want it filled.
  • Someone can take a call from a new enquiry between 9:00 and 19:00.
  • You would rather pay for a result than a promise.

Not a fit if

  • Nobody is free to pick up when a new enquiry comes in.
  • You want leads dropped in an inbox to deal with next week.
  • Your main aim is the lowest possible cost per lead.

Questions

Solar installer marketing, answered straight.

What does a solar installer marketing agency do?

It finds homeowners who want solar and gets them into your diary. Tract runs the Meta ads, phones every enquiry to check it is genuine, and books qualified homeowners into your calendar for a survey.

Do Meta ads work for solar installers?

They can. Meta lets you reach homeowners in the area you serve, and a fast phone call after the enquiry is what turns interest into a booked survey. Tract has booked 12 appointments in 10 days with Meta ads for a kitchen fitting franchise, and is now applying the same system to solar. Results depend on your area and how quickly surveys can be taken on, so you get a realistic range on the call, not a promise.

How much do solar installer leads cost with Tract?

You don't pay per lead or per click. You pay for attended surveys only, and Tract covers the ad spend. The rate is agreed on the call, based on your area and the work you take on.

What counts as an attended survey?

Your surveyor is at the property and the homeowner is there. If the visit doesn't happen for any reason, you aren't billed.

What do I need to do on my side?

Give Tract a calendar with real availability and tell it the area you cover. Someone on your side needs to be reachable between 9:00 and 19:00 when a new enquiry comes in.

Which areas do you cover?

Anywhere in the UK. Tell Tract the postcodes you serve and how far you will travel, and the ads and the vetting call are built around that.
