Where solar Meta ads go wrong

Four reasons most solar Facebook ad campaigns stall.

01

Optimised for leads, not surveys

A cheap lead that never picks up is expensive. Campaigns should be built around booked appointments.

02

Slow follow-up

Leads that wait a day or two for a call have often moved on. Speed to the first call decides who gets the survey.

03

No vetting

Tenants, people with no say in the decision and people outside your area waste a surveyor's slot.

04

Reporting on the wrong number

Cost per lead hides what matters. The number to watch is cost per attended survey.

Questions

Solar installer Meta ads, answered straight.

How do Meta ads work for solar installers?

Meta (Facebook and Instagram) lets you show ads to homeowners in the area you serve. A homeowner who is interested taps through and leaves their details or picks a time. What happens next decides whether it turns into a survey, so the follow-up matters as much as the ad.

Should I optimise solar Meta ads for leads or booked surveys?

Booked surveys. Optimising for form fills tends to bring people who never answer the phone. In campaigns Tract has run, optimising for scheduled appointments cost less per booked appointment than optimising for leads.

Why do solar leads from Facebook go cold?

Because nobody calls them quickly. A homeowner who submits a form and hears nothing for a day or two has often spoken to someone else. Speed to the first phone call is the biggest single factor.

Can I run Meta ads for solar myself?

Yes, and many installers do. The ads are the easier half. The harder half is calling every enquiry quickly, vetting it and getting it into the diary. That is the part Tract takes off your hands.

What does Tract charge for solar Meta ads?

There is no retainer and no ad spend charged to you. You pay for attended surveys only. The rate is agreed on the call.
