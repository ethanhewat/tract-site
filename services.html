Where we start

Reactivation done right. Not email marketing.

You've already paid to acquire every customer on your list. Most showrooms never contact them again after the sale — and furniture has a long buying cycle, which means most lists are full of people who are due to come back.

This isn't a newsletter tool bolted onto your CRM. It's not a "20% off everything" blast sent to your whole list on a Friday. That approach trains people to wait for a discount and slowly kills your deliverability.

What we actually do:

  • List segmentation — grouped by what they bought, when, and how they've engaged since, not blasted as one block.
  • Personalised sequences — messages that reference real purchase and enquiry history, written to read like a real conversation.
  • Attribution built in — you see exactly what revenue came from what send, not a vague open-rate number.
  • Ongoing list health — cleaned and re-segmented on a quarterly cycle so deliverability holds up over time.

Reactivation is the fastest lever available because there's no acquisition cost — the customer already exists. The only question is how much of that list is actually recoverable.

Paid media

Meta & Google Ads

Furniture is a considered purchase with a long buying cycle. Your ad strategy needs to match that — cold prospecting to build awareness, retargeting to bring people back when they're ready, and attribution so you know exactly what's working.

Organic

SEO

75% of furniture purchase decisions involve Google before a store is ever contacted. If you're not visible when someone searches "furniture showroom near me" or a specific product term, a competitor is.

We build out product and local pages, fix the technical basics, and grow your organic visibility over time — a channel that keeps compounding long after the work is done.
