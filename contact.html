How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on your list size and which services you need. We don't publish pricing upfront because it should reflect your specific situation — that's what the free revenue gap review is for.

Do I need an existing customer list for reactivation?

Yes. Reactivation works from an existing list of past customers or enquiries, even if it hasn't been touched in years. If you don't have a list yet, we'll talk through other channels instead.

How long until I see results?

Email reactivation campaigns typically show results within the first 1-2 weeks of launch. SEO and organic channels take longer, generally 3-6 months to build meaningful traction.
