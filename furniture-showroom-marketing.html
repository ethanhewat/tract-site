Why furniture is different

Generic agency playbooks don't fit a furniture showroom.

Most agencies market a showroom like an online shop: one campaign, a product photo, "Shop Now". That works for a phone case. It doesn't work for a £2,000 dining set.

01

A long buying cycle

Weeks or months between first look and purchase. Your marketing has to stay in front of people for that whole time, not just the first click.

02

The visit is the conversion

People want to sit on it, touch the fabric, see the colour in real light. The job is getting them through your door, not a checkout.

03

Customers come back

New home, new room, new baby, a spare room turned office. Past buyers are your best next buyers, if you stay in touch properly.

How we work with showrooms

Start with who you know. Then bring in who you don't.

01

Reactivate your past customers

Your customer list is the cheapest revenue you'll find. We clean and segment it by what people bought and when, then send personalised campaigns: a new collection preview for past sofa buyers, a dining range for people who bought a table five years ago. No discount blasts.

02

Meta and Google ads that bring people in

Geo-targeted to people who can realistically visit and take delivery. Prospecting to earn attention, retargeting to bring browsers back when they're ready, and tracking on booked visits and sales rather than clicks.

03

Show up when people search locally

"Furniture shop near me" and "[your town] furniture showroom" are searches from people ready to visit. We set up your Google Business Profile, reviews and local pages so you're the one they find.

04

Follow up without lifting a finger

Every enquiry gets a fast response and an easy way to book a showroom visit or home consultation, even at 9pm on a Sunday. Reminders cut no-shows, and nothing goes cold because nobody got back in time.

Questions

What showroom owners ask us.

Do you only work with furniture showrooms?

No. We work with independent home, garden and lifestyle brands, but furniture showrooms are where we started and where most of our thinking comes from. The long buying cycle, the showroom visit, the high order value: we build everything around that.

How big does my customer list need to be?

For reactivation, 2,000 contacts or more. If your list is smaller, we'll usually start with Meta ads and local search instead, and tell you honestly if that's the better route.

How quickly will we see results?

Reactivation is the fastest: campaigns can be live within about a week and responses start within days. Paid ads take a few weeks to settle, and local SEO builds over months. That's why we start with reactivation.

Do you work with online-only furniture brands?

Our focus is furniture retailers with a showroom or physical space, where the goal is getting people through the door. If you sell purely online, get in touch and we'll tell you straight whether we're a fit.

What does it cost?

It depends on your list size and which channels make sense. You'll get a clear number on a free 30-minute revenue gap review, with no obligation.
